# aaryan — Krishi Sales (Heera Krishak Bazar, Narsinghpur)

A simple sales tool for Heera Krishak Bazar, an agri-input shop in Narsinghpur (MP) selling seeds, fertilizer and pesticide
**wholesale (retailers, FPOs) and retail (farmers)**.
It runs in any browser, on a phone or a computer. There's nothing to install and no monthly fee.

## What it does

| Tab | What it helps with |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's and this month's sales, % of bills with a name + mobile, total udhaar, best-selling products, low stock, and a "who to contact today" list |
| **New Bill** | Make a bill. For a walk-in farmer, type name + mobile and they are saved as a customer automatically (no more nameless "Cash" bills). Wholesale customers automatically get wholesale prices. Unpaid amounts go to udhaar. Send the bill on WhatsApp in one tap |
| **Customers** | Farmers (retail) and shops or dealers (wholesale), with village, crops and land |
| **Products** | Retail price, wholesale price, buying cost (shows your margin), stock and low-stock alerts |
| **Udhaar** | Who owes how much. By default only retailers/FPOs can take credit; farmer credit can be turned on in Settings. Record payments. Send a polite WhatsApp reminder |
| **Sell More** | Shows what farmers need this season (Narsinghpur crop calendar: gram, wheat, masoor, sugarcane, tur, summer moong, maize, soybean). Pick a product and a crop, and send a ready-made Hindi or English offer to every matching customer on WhatsApp |
| **Settings** | Shop name, phone, message language, backup and restore |

## How to open it

- **On a computer:** download this repo and double-click `app/index.html`.
- **On a phone:** host the `app` folder for free (for example with GitHub Pages) and open the link.
  Then use your browser's "Add to Home screen" option.

Click **Load demo data** to try it out, then delete it from Settings and enter your real products and customers.

## Important

- Your data is saved **only in that browser on that device**. Go to Settings and use **Download backup**
  every week, and keep the file safe (for example on Google Drive or in WhatsApp to yourself).
- WhatsApp messages are opened for you to send yourself. Nothing is sent automatically, so your number
  stays safe from being blocked as spam. Only message customers who know your shop.
